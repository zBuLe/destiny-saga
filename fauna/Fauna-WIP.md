# Fantasy Fauna Descriptor Reference

This is a quick-reference list of the descriptors we’ve defined so far, grouped by what they measure.

## 1) Presence

- Apex: Typically stands its ground; hard to intimidate or displace. Applies to predators *and* prey. [Lion, Elephant]
- Stout: Can chase off weaker threats and contest space/food, but yields when something clearly stronger shows up. [Wolverine, Honey badger, Goat]
- Skittish: Hair-trigger exit behavior; if something feels wrong, it disengages fast (flee, curl, smoke, drop-tail, etc.). [Rabbit, Deer, Lemming]

## 2) Size 

- Critter: Hand-sized and smaller (up to house-kitten scale). [Rat, Sparrow, Insects]
- Creature: Cat-to-anteater scale. [Goat, Coyote, Snow leopard]
- Beast: Large but doesn’t tower over a person. [Deer, Bison, Bear]
- Theros: Towers over a person / megafauna presence. [Giraffe, Elephant, Whale, Brontosaur]

## 3) Environment

- Terrestrial: Lives on land; can enter water, but must return to land long-term. [Dog, Cat, Moose]
- Marine: Lives in water; can go on land, but must return to water long-term. [Fish, Seal, Whale, Sea turtle]
- Aquatic: Can live long-term on both land and water; commonly uses both. [Frog, Salamander, Crocodile, Hippo]

## 4) Locomotion
- Walker: Can locomote effectively on land. [Horse, Goat, Snake, Seal]
- Swimmer: Can locomote effectively in water. [Fish, Penguin, Bear]
- Flyer: Can locomote through air. [Bat, Duck, Flying fish]


## 5) Diet 

- Carnivore: Eats other animals. [Lion, Crocodile, Orca]
- Herbivore: Eats plants. [Goat, Deer, Rabbit]
- Omnivore: Eats both animals and plants. [Raccoon, Bear, Pig]
- Lumenvore: Gains nutrition from sunlight (photosynthesis / “solar feeding”). [Chikorita (Pokémon), Sun-sail lizard (fantasy), Lumen grazer (fantasy)]
- Lithivore: Can ingest inorganic material (rocks, dirt, clay) as meaningful food or supplement. [Gorons (Zelda), Goat (clay/salt), Rock-eater beetle (fantasy)]

## 6) Feeding

- Hunter: Seeks out live prey. [Lion, Wolf, Crocodile]
- Scavenger: Seeks out dead animals / leftovers. [Vulture, Hyena]
- Grazer: Feeds across vast edible surfaces or dense “food fields.” [Cow, Bison, Baleen whale]
- Browser: Selective harvesting from patches (leaves/shrubs/shoots). [Goat, Giraffe]
- Forager: Searches for scattered edible tidbits. [Raccoon, Boar]
- Parasite: Feeds from a living host over time. [Tick, Lamprey]
- Basker: Stays still to gain nutrition (usually tied to lumenvore “charging”). [Sea Sponge, Chikorita (Pokémon), Sun-basking lumenvore (fantasy)]

## 7) Activity

- Diurnal: Awake during the day. [Eagle, Zebra]
- Nocturnal: Awake at night. [Bat, Owl]
- Cathemeral: Active and resting throughout both day and night. [Lion, Domestic cat]

## 8) Human relationship (people-centered tag)

- Pest: Causes problems to people. [Raccoon, Rat, Locust]
- Boon** *(opposite of pest)*: Generally helpful/beneficial to people (pollination, cleanup, pest-control, protection, etc.). [Bee, Ladybug, Vulture]

## 9) Origin 

- Native: From that place. [Local deer, Local songbird]
- Invasive: Brought into that region and spreads/causes disruption. [Island rats, Rabbits in Australia]

## 10) Grouping

- Solitary: Lives alone; one parent raises young or young are on their own early. [Polar bear, Tiger]
- Partner: Mated pair together; may raise young together. [Swans, Eagles]
- Pack: Small coordinated group; members need not be related. [Wolf, Wild dog]
- Tribe: Small coordinated group with complex roles (sentry/foragers/soldiers/etc.); members are related. [Monkeys, Meerkats]
- Herd: Large multi-family group; individuals still matter; group defense is a thing. [Zebra, Bison]
- Flock: Large group where the group matters more than the individual; stragglers are abandoned. [Migratory geese, Starlings, Fish Schools]
- Swarm: Undifferentiated mass; the individual doesn’t matter; loss doesn’t change group behavior. [Locusts, Krill]

## 11) Reproduction

- Born: Live birth. [Kangaroo, Horse]
- Hatch: Egg hatch. [Platypus, Sea turtle]

## 12) Year-scale pattern

- Yearround: Active year-round; no major seasonal phase-shift. [Dog, Deer]
- Seasonal: Strong changes by season (coat, migration, mating, etc.). [Arctic fox, Emperor penguin]
- Hibernating: Sleeps through a season. [Bear, Groundhog]
- Emergent: Long inactivity with sudden periodic surges. [Cicadas]

## 13) Outer covering

- Furred: Covered in fur. [Dog, Cat]
- Feathered: Covered in feathers. [Eagle, Duck]
- Scaled: Covered in scales. [Snake, Salmon]
- Plated: Solid armor-like surface. [Turtle, Armadillo, Pangolin]
- Slick: Mucus/goo coated. [Frog, Salamander]
- Bald: Exposed skin. [Human, Sphynx cat, Vulture head]

## 14) range

- Territorial: Has a defined defended area. [Lion, Goose (nest season)]
- Roaming: Moves about without a defended territory. [Goat, Elephant]
- Nesting: Has a place to be (nest/burrow/hive/den). [Bees, Prairie dogs, Most birds]
- Migrant: Moves to far-away places (regular long-distance movement). [Migratory birds, Whales]

## 15) Temperament (approach vs fight/flee balance)

- Monster: Will approach and fight people (human-targeting threat). [Godzilla, Jason, Kraken, Curse-wolf ghost]
- Hostile: Won’t approach; if provoked/approached, more likely to fight than flee . [Goose, Hippo, Bear]
- Bold: Will approach/intrude; may fight or flee depending on pushback. [Raccoon, Monkey]
- Placid: Won’t approach; can be approached; holds ground and chases off if annoyed. [Manatee, Baleen whale]
- Docile: Will approach and can be approached; high tolerance; won’t fight or flee even under rough handling. [Horse, Dog]
- Wary: May approach cautiously, but flees when spotted/pressed. [Coyote, Deer]
- Flee: Won’t approach; won’t tolerate approach; flees rather than fights. [Rabbit]

- Cornered: If escape is impossible, almost any temperament can flip into fighting.

## 16) Visibility strategy (detection & signaling)

- Brazen: Doesn’t hide; moves openly with little concern for who’s watching. [Bear, Hyena, Manatee]
- Elusive: Hard to detect by default due to build (quiet movement, camouflage, low visual signature). [Tiger, Owl, Leopard, Zebra]
- Covert: Actively maintains concealment while moving; behavior-driven hiding/mimicry. [Stick bug, Octopus, Meerkat (when hiding/peeking), Chameleon]
- Blaring: Makes presence known through conspicuous display/sound/color; “notice me.” [Wasp, Skunk, Coral snake, Guard dog (barking), Peacock]
