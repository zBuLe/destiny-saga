Life/ Wander --- Death/ Absolute
one wanders thru life, but death is absolute.
Wander refers to be able to go here, or there, or over there. to not stay still, not stay in one place. endless possibilities  
Absolute refers that no matter what, this is, this will be, will always arrive. no matter where you go, you will arrive at one place. One possibility

Strength/ Integrity --- Wisdom/ Discovery
To be strong and remain as one, to be wise and see the many
Strength refers to be able to withstand, to endure, to remain undivided, to remain unchanged when it mattes most. to have integrity
Wisdom refers to see the difference, to compare and contrast, to discerned, to perceive. to change oneself when needed. to discover oneself anew

Dark/ Egoism --- Light/ Altruism
For the benefit of all, I must be at my best. To be my best, I must benefit all
Dark refers to placing the focus of one's actions upon oneself. to improve, to bring the best of my self, if i fall, i pick myself up. to be honest with my self, true to myself
Light refers to placing the focus of one's actions upon others. to improve others, to bring the best of others. if someone falls, i pick them up, to be honest with others, true to others

Harmony / Tolerance --- Chaos/ Freedom
To deal with others, one must know where they stand. I am here, deal with it.

Harmony works well with other they know their own boundaries and respect each other's boundaries. They know the tolerances needed to be in harmony with each other. 

Chaos is when everyone goes their own way. Disregarding whatever boundaries they cross. And understand other may cross theirs. They don't let anyone limit their path, and respect when other won't be limited by them.

Expanding a bit more. In a sound harmony, the different notes to play a harmony work well with each other. A different note would sound discordant. The range, the boundaries of each note mater so they harmonize. The mechanical tolerance. How much give can each part have for the machine to work. A high tolerance part can tolerate more imperfections before it fails, and a low tolerance part would need to be flawless parts to keep working

To contrast with chaos freedom. First, in an mob, organized crime society, the boundaries the law imposes can be crossed. But a way to think off it is gas particles. They travel in in any direction. They are free to do so. They collide. But that is how it is, everyone can move their own way, sooner or later, there will be a collision. But the collision still happens, there is some heat, and their paths change. Just the bigger one is mostly unchanged and the smaller one changes the most. Again with the mob framing, it's not personal, it's business.

It is about minding others. Harmony, A machine parts minds other parts of the machine. The part even has space for other parts to fit. And if parts cross each other boundaries, a bent pin hitting against an axel, then the machine comes apart. Or in gas particle metaphor, laminar flow. 
With chaos, particles aren't minding each other, they collide

Solitude/ Endurance --- Communal/ Reliable
I must do all things, i shouldn't rely on others. I just need to do my part, i can rely on others
Solitude is being able to be alone. to know what needs to known. to do what needs to done, so others don't need to do it for me. 
Communal is being able be with others. to know your part, to do your part, you do your part for others, and others do their part for you
to expand, it revolves on the capabilities of the one v the group. it isn't that the solitary cant be relied on, they most likely be a great person to rely on. but for them, they don't seek to rely on others, there is a helplessness when others help them. communal doesn't mean they cant endure, but they shouldn't on their own, they can share the load. they can help on this so that someone else can help on that. they feel helpless ness when they cant help others.

Simple/ Forward --- Complex/ Lateral
what's to think about? its is straight ahead. Come to think of it, it can be approached in another way
Simple is to be forward, direct, to just go there. to not be bothered by other thing not present, not relevant
Complex is to think lateral, to consider the approach, to contemplate the possibilities before executing. to weight in what is imperceptible, what is relevant

Fire/ Radiance - - - Wind/ Direction

Radience is like fire, it spreads everywhere. It's light, it's warmth, it's burn. Wherever it can reach, it will reach. Like a smile and happiness, like a cry and sadness. Emotion spreads within oneself and to others.

Direction is like the winds, it moves from here to there. A breeze, a gust, a storm. From here to there, like breath, like a thought, which directs the arm to move, the legs to run. A force to direct, to command yourself and others from here to there

---

Earth/ Firmness - - - Water/ Fluidity
Firmness is to be like Earth, to be able to hold. to stay in whatever form, like a mountain, a rock, sand. to stay together or fracture. to hold shape despite the movement, like bones and muscle
Fluidity is to be like Water, to be able to flow. to move in whichever way, like a lake, river, rapids. in laminar or turbulent movement. to be shaped by moments, like sweat and blood

---

Ice/ Matter - - - Lightning/ Action
What holds and flow, Matter. what spreads and directs, action
Matter is what exist. what can be touched, held, moved. it is the body. it is what IS acted upon
Energy is to be acts. The movement, speed, change. it is the mind. it is what CAN act upon
